DeeMarine Insight of the Week

Infrastructure alone is no longer enough.

Across Edition 1, the strongest pattern is that maritime competitiveness is being built as a connected system: physical capacity, digital visibility, security intelligence, alternative fuels, inland connectivity and trade facilitation are moving together. DeeMarine’s framework is: Capacity + Throughput + Turnaround + Digitalization + Security/Risk + Total Logistics Cost.
